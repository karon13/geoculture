Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

StoryGlobe has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name or email, and the game itself does not send your guesses or results to any server of ours.

What is saved on your device

The game saves your results for each day, your streak and your choice of kilometres or miles in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device. It is not sent anywhere, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Outside services your browser contacts

To show the map and the pictures, your browser loads files directly from other services. Like any website you visit, those services can see your IP address and basic browser information when this happens, and they have their own privacy policies.

  • OpenFreeMap provides the map style and map tiles.
  • Wikimedia Commons and, for a few pictures, public museum and library collections, provide the pictures shown with the stories.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and receives the usual web server logs.

Advertising and analytics

At the moment StoryGlobe shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes, for example if we add advertising, this page will be updated first to say which partners are involved, what they collect, and how you can choose.
