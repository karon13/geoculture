    How to play

    Every day there are five prompts from art, music, books and movies. Each one comes from a real place somewhere in the world. Your job: find it on the globe.

    Playing a round

    1. Read the prompt and the tag (art, music, books or movies).
    2. Spin and zoom the globe, then tap where you think it comes from. Tap again to move your pin.
    3. Press Lock in guess. You see the real place, how far off you were, and the story behind it.

    Scoring

    Each round is worth up to 100 points, 500 a day. A guess within 25 km (15 miles) earns the full 100. After that the points fall off quickly: about 57 at 1,000 km (620 miles) and about 7 at 5,000 km (3,100 miles). There are no labels on the map, so it is down to what you know.

    Every day, new places

    A new set of five appears at midnight, in your own time zone. Everyone who plays on the same date gets the same five, so you can compare scores. When you finish, use Share result to send your squares to a friend.

    Streak and past days

    Play today to keep your daily streak going. You can also catch up on the last 28 days from Previous results in the menu. Those games are fun to play but only today counts toward your streak.

    Good to know

    Your progress is saved in this browser only, so clearing site data or switching browsers starts you from scratch. Distances show in kilometres or miles; change that in the menu.
